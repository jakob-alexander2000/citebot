# Time off and leave

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive **30 days** of paid vacation per calendar year, plus German public holidays observed in Berlin.

Part-time employees receive PTO pro-rated by FTE.

Unused PTO does not carry over past March 31 of the following year unless a manager approves an exception in writing.

## Requesting leave

Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least **10 working days** before the first day off. For absences longer than five consecutive days, give **20 working days** notice when possible.

Managers should respond within two working days. Conflicts are resolved by seniority on the project, then by who requested first.

## Sick leave

Notify your manager and `#ops` in Slack before 09:30 on the day you are out. For absences longer than three consecutive calendar days, a doctor's note is required.

## Parental leave

We follow German statutory parental leave (Elternzeit). Talk to People Ops at least eight weeks before your planned start so payroll and coverage can be arranged.
