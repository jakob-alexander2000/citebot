# Friday demo day

Every Friday at 15:00 Europe/Berlin we run a short internal demo.

## Format

- Five minutes per slot, hard stop
- Show something running — a URL, a CLI, or a local build
- End with one sentence on what broke and what you learned

## Who presents

Anyone who shipped that week. If you have nothing to show, say so in `#demo` by Thursday noon so we can shorten the agenda.

## Guests

External guests are welcome when the presenter opts in. Recordings stay internal unless the presenter marks the slot as shareable.
