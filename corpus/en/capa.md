# Corrective and preventive action (CAPA)

Nonconformities at Hartmann Fertigung GmbH are tracked in the CAPA log. An action is closed only after cause and effectiveness have been checked.

## Opening a record

A CAPA record is opened in the CAPA log within one working day of detection. The person who found the issue names the part, order, or batch and the immediate observation. Guesses about the cause do not belong in the first entry.

## Classes and containment

Class A nonconformities are customer complaints or safety-related defects. They require containment within 24 hours. Class B is an internal nonconformity with no customer impact and requires containment within 3 working days. Containment quarantines or identifies the affected parts before root-cause work starts.

## Root cause and effectiveness

A 5-Why or Ishikawa root-cause analysis is required before a corrective action is closed. The effectiveness check takes place 90 calendar days after implementation. If the same class A cause recurs within 12 months, the case is escalated to the management review.
