# Suppliers

Series suppliers to Hartmann Fertigung GmbH are listed on the approved supplier list. Purchasing places series orders only with approved suppliers. This chapter is the binding FAQ for approval and scoring.

## Approval

Series suppliers must be on the approved supplier list before the first series order. A new series supplier needs a self-assessment and an approved initial sample (EMPB) before the first series delivery. Sample parts without EMPB approval stay quarantined.

## Scoring

Re-evaluation is annual, or sooner after two class A complaints within 12 months. The scale runs from 0 to 100 and combines delivery reliability and quality performance. A score below 80 requires a supplier development plan within 30 days. A score below 60 blocks new orders until quality and purchasing lift the block together.

## Evidence

At approval and at each re-evaluation, a current ISO 9001 certificate or an equivalent system certificate is requested. A missing certificate does not by itself block an order already in progress, but it is a finding in the re-evaluation.
