# Quality policy

Hartmann Fertigung GmbH machines precision turned and milled parts in Bielefeld for machinery customers. This chapter is the binding quality policy of the quality handbook. It describes the quality management system along the lines of ISO 9001:2015 and is not itself a certificate.

## Approval and management review

Executive management releases the quality objectives in the first-quarter management review. The current issue carries the approval date 12 March 2026. Each manager makes sure their area works to the released objectives and sends nonconformities to the CAPA log.

## Objectives

The customer-complaint target is at most 8 ppm over a rolling 12-month window. Internal scrap must not exceed 1.2% of parts produced in a month. The quality representative reviews both figures monthly and reports them to executive management.

## Process stop

Any employee may stop a process when a suspected nonconforming part could reach the customer. The process stop is reported to the shift lead at once and recorded in the shift log during the same shift. The shift lead releases the restart only after the parts are quarantined and identified.
