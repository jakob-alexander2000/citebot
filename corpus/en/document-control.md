# Document control

Controlled documents at Hartmann Fertigung GmbH live in the QM portal. Only the revision released there is binding. This chapter covers change, distribution, and retention.

## Current revisions

Printouts are uncontrolled copies and must be checked against the QM portal before use. Anyone working from an obsolete copy stops the job and retrieves the released revision. Drafts are marked “in review” in the portal and must not be left in the workshop.

## Changes

Change requests go to the quality representative. After release, affected departments are notified within 2 working days. Training needs are recorded in the change log. A superseded revision is archived within 2 working days and marked “obsolete” in the portal.

## Retention

Controlled records (training, internal audits, nonconformities) are kept for at least 5 years. Customer-specific inspection records and initial sample reports (EMPB) are kept for 10 years. The period starts on the date the record was released.
