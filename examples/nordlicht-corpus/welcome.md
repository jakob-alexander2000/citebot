# Welcome to Nordlicht Labs

Nordlicht Labs is a small product studio based in Berlin. We build internal tools and AI demos for European teams that care about clarity over hype.

## Mission

Ship small, sharp products. Prefer a working demo over a pitch deck. If it is on the site, it runs.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00 Europe/Berlin**, Monday through Friday. Outside that window, async is the default. Nobody is expected to respond to Slack after 19:00 local time.

## Office

We are hybrid. Desk days are Tuesday and Thursday at our Kreuzberg studio (near Kottbusser Tor). Remote days are Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. Visiting founders and freelancers need a calendar invite before showing up on desk days.

## First week checklist

1. Complete onboarding in Notion (IT, payroll, tools).
2. Pair with your buddy on a tiny shippable change.
3. Present that change in Friday demo (5 minutes max).
