# Expense policy

## What we reimburse

- Work travel booked through the company travel desk
- Client meals up to **€45 per person** with a receipt and attendees listed
- Home-office equipment up to **€400 per year** (chair, monitor, keyboard) with prior manager approval
- Conference tickets when approved in Linear under the Learning budget

## What we do not reimburse

- Commuting within Berlin on desk days
- Alcohol beyond one drink on a client meal
- Personal software subscriptions already covered by company licenses

## How to submit

Upload receipts to the expense tool within **14 days**. Include project code when the cost is billable to a client. Finance pays approved expenses every second Thursday.
